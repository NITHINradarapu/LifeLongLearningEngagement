1. In-Video Active Conceptual Checkpoints Cognitive Engagement
Backend: SQLite `checkpoints`, `checkpoint_attempts` | Frontend: HTML5/JS Player Event Listeners
Instead of passive video streaming, interactive questions pause playback at predefined timestamps (`timestamp_seconds`). Learners must actively recall concepts before continuing, eliminating video fatigue and verifying comprehension in real time.
2. Generative AI Adaptive Assessment Engine AI / NLP Innovation
Engine: Google GenAI (Gemini 2.0 Flash) | Validation: Pydantic Structured Outputs (`AIQuiz`, `AIQuestion`)
Dynamically generates targeted quizzes based on course topics and target difficulty. Unlike static question banks, each question includes rich learning objectives, verified options, and pedagogical explanations stored in relational tables.
3. Micro-Topic Mastery & Skill Gap Diagnostics Learning Analytics
Table: `learner_topic_performance` (Tracks accuracy, attempts, and mastery per concept)
The system categorizes topic knowledge into Developing, Proficient, and Mastered. Students and faculty can instantly pinpoint exact conceptual gaps rather than relying on an ambiguous overall course grade.
4. Goal-Aligned Dynamic Learning Paths Personalization
Table: `learning_path_items` with priority queues and student career targets
Adapts course sequences based on the learner's declared career goal (e.g. AI Engineer, Web Developer) and current skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced), preventing catalog overload.
5. Daily Habit Formation & Gamification Loop Behavioral Retention
Tables: `daily_learning_goals`, `gamification_events`, `users.streak`
Tracks daily learning target minutes, checkpoint goals, streak counters, and awarded points for consistency. This replicates proven habit-formation mechanics (like Duolingo) to combat adult learner procrastination.
6. Educator Monitoring & AI Auditing Infrastructure System Governance
Tables: `ai_generation_logs`, `messages`, `notifications`, Admin Student Drill-Downs
Faculty have full observability: detailed student drill-down analytics, two-way query messaging, and comprehensive AI generation audit logs that track model prompts, status, and error prevention.

★ Bonus: Rapid-Fire Panel Questions & Teammate Defense Cheatsheet

Q: How do you quantitatively measure "Engagement"?

A: Through three concrete metrics in SQLite: (1) Attendance & Daily Streaks (frequency of return), (2) Video Checkpoint Completion Ratio (active watch time vs passive drop-off), and (3) Assessment Attempt Latency & Accuracy Trajectory over consecutive sessions.

Q: How do you prevent AI Hallucinations in assessments?

A: We use Google GenAI with strict Pydantic schema enforcement (`AIQuiz`, `AIQuestion`). The model is restricted to a structured JSON response specifying the question, 4 distinct options, verified single-letter answer (A-D), and objective explanation, which is validated before insertion into SQLite.

Q: Why SQLite instead of MongoDB or PostgreSQL?

A: SQLite offers relational ACID compliance, foreign-key cascade guarantees (`ON DELETE CASCADE`), zero latency, and zero configuration for portable demonstrations. In our production roadmap, it smoothly ports to PostgreSQL via SQLAlchemy or direct SQL migration.

Q: What is the future scope of this research project?

A: Incorporating predictive machine learning models (Random Forest / Logistic Regression) to compute an automated Early Dropout Risk Score (0-100%) based on learner engagement decay, alerting educators proactively before students disengage.
